Rules and privacy

Who can enter

Students in kindergarten through 12th grade who live in Bellevue, Washington, or attend a school in the Bellevue School District. One entry per student. No teams. Entering is free.

Students can submit their own entry. An adult may help with the form.

What to submit

  • An essay about one idea that would make Bellevue better, within the word limit for the student's grade.
  • One piece of art that shows the idea, uploaded as a single image.
  • The city department the idea is addressed to.

Deadline: .

Original work

The essay and art must be the student's own. An adult may type for a student in kindergarten through 2nd grade, using the student's words. AI tools may not write the essay or make the art. Entries that copy someone else's work will be removed.

How entries are judged

The organizers check each entry for eligibility and remove names before judging. Entries that include personal attacks or unsafe content are not put on the ballot.

Judges are members of the public, 18 or older, with a confirmed email address. Each judge gets one winner pick and one runner-up pick per grade. Judges are asked four questions:

  1. Is the problem real, and is it explained clearly?
  2. Could the city carry out the idea?
  3. Does the art help you understand it?
  4. Does it sound like the student's own thinking?

A winner pick counts for 2 points and a runner-up pick for 1. In each grade, the entry with the most points wins and the next entry is runner-up. Ties go to the entry with more winner picks. If it is still tied, the organizers decide.

Judges sit out any grade where a family member entered. Votes that look coordinated or duplicated may be thrown out.

Prizes

Each grade has one winner and one runner-up. Scholarship amounts depend on sponsor support and will be posted before submissions open, along with how they are paid. For winners under 18, a parent or guardian signs off before a prize is paid.

Winners are invited to present their ideas to city staff and the Bellevue City Council. Presentations depend on the city's schedule and agreement, and are not guaranteed.

Privacy

We collect the student's name, grade and school, and a contact email, so that we can run the contest and reach winners. We do not sell or share this information.

Judges see the entry number, grade, title, essay and art. They do not see names, schools or contact details. Please keep the student's name and face out of the essay and the art photo.

A winner's first name, grade and school are published only if that box was checked on the entry form, and only after we confirm with a parent or guardian. A student, parent or guardian can ask us to withdraw and delete an entry at any time.

Who runs this

is an independent community contest. It is not run, sponsored or endorsed by the Bellevue School District or the City of Bellevue. The organizers may change dates or rules if needed and will post changes on this page.
