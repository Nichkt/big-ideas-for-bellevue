How it works

  1. Pick one thing to fix or build

    Something you see on the way to school, at a park, or on the bus. A small, specific idea beats a big, vague one.

  2. Write the essay

    Say what the problem is, what you would do about it, and who it helps. Word limits depend on your grade.

  3. Make the art

    A drawing, painting, poster, model or photo that shows the idea. You upload a picture of it.

  4. Send it in

    Fill out the entry form yourself, or ask an adult to help. One entry per student.

What winners get

Each grade from kindergarten through 12th has one winner and one runner-up. That is 26 students.

A scholarship. Prizes are paid for by local sponsors. Amounts will be posted here before submissions open.

A seat at the table. Winners are invited to present their idea to the city department it is addressed to, and to the Bellevue City Council. We are asking the City of Bellevue to host these presentations, and this page will say so once it is confirmed.

    Questions

    Who can enter?

    Any student in kindergarten through 12th grade who lives in Bellevue or attends a school in the Bellevue School District. Private school and homeschool students who live in Bellevue can enter too.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. There is no fee to enter or to judge.

    How much can an adult help?

    Adults can talk through ideas, type for a student in kindergarten through 2nd grade, take the photo of the art, and help with the entry form. The words and the art have to be the student's own. AI tools may not write the essay or make the art.

    What makes a strong entry?

    A real problem, explained clearly. An idea the city could carry out. Art that helps a stranger understand it. Judges are asked to score for the grade level, so a first grader is never compared with a senior.

    Can students enter as a team?

    No. Each entry comes from one student, and each student can send one entry.

    Who sees the student's name?

    Only the organizers. Judges see an entry number, the grade, the essay and the art. Winners' names are published only with permission.
