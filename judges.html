Community judging

Be a judge

Anyone 18 or older can judge. You get one ballot, with a winner pick and a runner-up pick in each grade from kindergarten through 12th.

How judging works

Sign up with your email. We send a link to confirm it is you, and that link opens your ballot. One email address is one judge.

Voting dates: . You can vote in as many grades as you have time for, and change your picks until voting closes.

Entries are shown by number. Names and schools are hidden. If someone in your family entered, tell us the grade below and that grade's ballot will be closed to you.

What to look for

  1. Is the problem real, and is it explained clearly?
  2. Could the city carry out the idea?
  3. Does the art help you understand it?
  4. Does it sound like the student's own thinking?

Judge each entry against others in the same grade. Neat handwriting and expensive art supplies should not decide it.
